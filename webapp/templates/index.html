Clinical Research Methodology:

  • Dataset: SkinDisNet (1,710 verified clinical preprocessed photographs across 6 diagnostic categories).
  • Patient Partitioning: Stratified patient-level splitting (292 train, 62 validation, 62 test patients) ensuring no cross-set patient leakage.
  • Backbone: ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNetB0 with two-stage training (Feature Extraction with frozen backbone followed by upper-layer fine-tuning).
  • Loss Weighting: Balanced inverse frequency class weights to address natural clinical class imbalance.
